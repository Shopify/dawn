# LP-3B.4 Merchant Operational Approval Register

## Cancellation Operations

Internal baseline: cancellation is most likely before a supplier order is placed. After supplier commitment, cancellation depends on supplier status and cannot be guaranteed. This remains an operating rule, not a legal conclusion.

| Paid and under merchant review | Hold supplier action while the cancellation request is assessed. | Merchant input still required | Confirm operational hold rule. |
| Paid; no CJ order placed | Cancellation is normally possible where operationally feasible after payment verification and confirmation that no supplier order exists. | Approved with amendment | Merchant adopted the feasibility qualification. |
