## Theme Editor Steps

1. Upload/select the three hero images in the `Get Yours promo grid hero` section.
2. Assign each category block to the matching collection.
3. Assign each product rail to the matching collection.
4. Keep the homepage product rails collection-driven. Do not hard-code product image assets into Liquid.
