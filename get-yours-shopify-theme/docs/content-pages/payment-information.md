# Payment Information

## Paying For Your Order

Available payment methods are shown at Shopify checkout before you place an order. Get Yours plans to use PayFast as the primary payment provider at launch.

[CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH] Confirm PayPal status before mentioning it publicly.

## Secure Checkout

Payment is processed through the available payment provider and Shopify checkout. Get Yours does not ask customers to send card details by email, social media or the contact form.

## Failed Or Cancelled Payments

If your payment fails or is cancelled, your order may not be completed. Check the details with your payment provider, then try checkout again if appropriate.

If you received an order number or payment reference and need help, contact Customer Service with those details.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are handled through the original payment method where possible, subject to the final Returns & Refunds information and policy and payment-provider processes.
