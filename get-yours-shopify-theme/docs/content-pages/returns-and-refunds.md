# Returns & Refunds

## Contact Us First

If there is a problem with an item, please contact Get Yours before returning it or requesting a refund. We will review the order details and explain the available next steps.

Please do not send an item back before receiving return instructions from Get Yours.

## What To Send Us

Please include:

- Your order number.
- The name and email address used at checkout.
- The product name and a short description of the issue.
- Clear photos if an item arrived damaged, incorrect or incomplete.

## Damaged Or Incorrect Items

Contact us as soon as possible if an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered. Photos of the item and packaging may help us assess the issue and advise on the next steps.
