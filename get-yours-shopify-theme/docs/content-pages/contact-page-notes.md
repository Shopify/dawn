# Contact Page Improvement Notes

The existing contact page should remain simple: name, email, phone number, message and one clear Send action.

## Before Publishing

- Add the approved support email: [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH] Confirm support email address.
- Add approved support hours: [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH] Confirm support hours.
- Confirm the Shopify contact-form recipient inbox is monitored.
- Add calm response-time wording only after the operational target is confirmed: [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH] Confirm response-time wording.

## Helpful Guidance Near The Form

Ask customers to include:

- Their order number, if applicable.
- The name and email address used at checkout.
- The product name and a short description of the issue.
- Photos for damaged or incorrect items where relevant.
