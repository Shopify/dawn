# Community story layout

The community rail repeats this seven-block order:

1. Wide written testimonial.
2. Small written testimonial beneath the wide one, left.
3. Small written testimonial beneath the wide one, right.
4. Portrait video.
5. Small written testimonial at the top of a stacked pair.
6. Small written testimonial at the bottom of that pair.
7. Portrait video.

Use the story blocks in the Theme Editor to edit and reorder content. Place video
stories in slots 4 and 7. Up to two complete patterns are supported. The existing
uploaded video remains in the first portrait slot; new content stays unpublished
until its content and consent checkbox is enabled. No ratings or verification
badges are generated automatically. Ratings default to hidden and verification defaults to off.

The rail cycles complete groups without cloning videos. Hover, keyboard focus,
manual scrolling and video playback pause movement. Reduced-motion and Theme
Editor sessions start paused. App blocks disable automatic cycling. Native
horizontal scrolling remains available without JavaScript.

On mobile the three-quote group fits within one rail panel, followed by portrait
video and stacked quote panels. Long quotes scroll within their focusable card;
video controls, transcripts and related product links remain available.

## Ratings, verification and product overlays

Each story block has a customer star rating (0 hides it; half-star increments
are supported) and a separate manual Verified checkbox. Enter only the actual
customer rating, and enable verification only when supported by evidence. Both
remain hidden for unapproved stories. These controls do not verify purchases
automatically or create aggregate review structured data.

Select Related product and enable the portrait overlay toggle to show a linked
product image, title and current Shopify price above the video controls. The
overlay appears only for approved portrait media. Disable the toggle to keep the
normal product link below the story. No duplicate link is rendered when the
overlay is active. Videos keep native playback controls and transcript support.
